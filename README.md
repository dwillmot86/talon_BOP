# Talon: Birds of Prey

A browser-based 3D bird-of-prey RPG. Play as a peregrine falcon in a British valley: ride thermals, stoop on wood pigeons and teal, and feed your chicks at the eyrie.

## Running it

`index.html` is a page fragment, without its own `<html>`, `<head>` or `<body>` tags. It's published as a Claude artifact, which wraps it in a full page. To play it locally, put it inside a minimal HTML page and serve that page over HTTP, then open it in a browser. Three.js loads from the jsDelivr CDN.

## Controls

- **Keyboard:** A/D to turn, W/S for nose down/up, Space to flap, Shift to stoop, E to eat, V to change camera, Esc to pause.
- **Touch:** a joystick to steer, plus FLAP, DIVE, EAT and VIEW buttons.
- **Gamepad:** supported.
